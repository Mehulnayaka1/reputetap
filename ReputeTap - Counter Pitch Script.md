# ReputeTap: "3-Second Counter Demo" Walk-In Pitch Script

## Target Prospect Profile
- **High-Volume Walk-In Local Businesses**: Specialty Cafés, Hair & Beauty Salons, Premium Car Spas, Aesthetic Dental & Diagnostic Clinics, Boutique Retail.
- **Timing**: Visit during off-peak hours:
  - **Cafés**: 3:00 PM – 5:00 PM
  - **Salons & Spas**: Tuesday–Thursday afternoon
  - **Car Spas**: Morning before 11:00 AM
  - **Clinics**: 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM (lunch break window)

---

## The 3-Step Walk-In Protocol

### Phase 1: The Disarming Entry (30 Seconds)
*Walk directly to the billing counter or ask for the manager/owner.*

> **You**: "Hi, good afternoon! Are you the owner or manager here?"  
> **Owner**: "Yes, I am the owner / manager. How can I help?"  
> **You**: "I'm not here to sell you social media management or a ₹30,000 monthly agency retainer. I just have a quick 10-second question: If I tap my phone on your counter right now, how easy is it for me to leave you a 5-star Google review?"  
> **Owner**: "Well, you have to search us on Google Maps, find our name, and scroll down to reviews..."

---

### Phase 2: The 3-Second Visual Hook
*Pull out a pre-branded sample acrylic standee from your bag and place it on the counter.*

> **You**: "Watch this. Take your phone out—iPhone or Android, unlock it, and just tap the back of your phone right here on this standee."  
> *(Owner taps their phone. In 0.5 seconds, the ReputeTap micro-landing page pops up showing their business name, 5 stars, and instant review tags).*  
> **Owner**: "Whoa, that was instant!"  
> **You**: "Exactly. 95% of your happy customers walk out without reviewing because searching and typing takes too long. With ReputeTap:
> 1. **Instant Tap/Scan**: No app installation, works on all modern smartphones via NFC or QR code.
> 2. **1-Tap AI Review Drafter**: Customers tap two tags like *'Great ambiance'* and *'Best coffee in town'*. It automatically writes a polished 2-sentence review, copies it, and jumps directly into your Google Maps review modal.
> 3. **Private Manager Resolution**: If someone had a bad experience, instead of venting publicly on Google Maps, they tap *'Speak with Management'*. It sends a private WhatsApp alert directly to your phone with their name and number so you can resolve the issue before a 1-star review is posted."

---

### Phase 3: The Low-Friction Closing Offer
> **Owner**: "How much does something like this cost?"  
> **You**: "Local agencies charge ₹20,000/month for general SEO retainers. The regular price for ReputeTap is a one-time ₹2,999 for 2 custom acrylic standees with zero monthly fees.
> 
> However, as part of our Vadodara Launch Sprint for the first 100 local businesses, we are delivering the complete setup—2 custom laser-cut acrylic standees with your logo, high-speed NFC chips, and lifetime digital routing—for just ₹299 total! Zero monthly software fees, zero apps to install.
> 
> If you'd like, I can have your two customized standees delivered and set up on your counter this week. Should we get your logo printed on matte black or crystal clear acrylic?"

## Objection Handling Guide

| Objection | Recommended Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"Our customers don't know how to use NFC."** | *"That's why we print a high-contrast QR code right below the NFC target. If someone has an older phone or NFC disabled, they just scan with their camera."* |
| **"Is this allowed by Google's review policy?"** | *"Yes! Google forbids blocking negative reviews from reaching Google. ReputeTap includes a direct, transparent public Google review button so it is 100% compliant with Google Business Profile policies."* |
| **"Can customers tamper with or re-program the chip?"** | *"No, we permanently lock the NTAG213 chip memory using cryptographic write-locks before delivery. It cannot be altered."* |
| **"What if we change our WhatsApp number later?"** | *"The standee uses dynamic parameterized routing. You can update your phone number or Google Place ID in your dashboard anytime without replacing the standees."* |
