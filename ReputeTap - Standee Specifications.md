# ReputeTap Physical Standee Specifications & Fabricator Brief

## 1. Physical & Material Specifications
- **Material**: 3mm to 4mm High-Gloss Cast Clear Acrylic (A-grade virgin acrylic sheet).
- **Format**: L-Shape or Slanted T-Shape Countertop Desk Standee.
- **Dimensions**:
  - **Width**: 100 mm (4.0 inches)
  - **Height**: 150 mm (6.0 inches)
  - **Base Return**: 50 mm slanted base (engineered for optimal 75° viewing angle at POS cash counters).
- **Printing Method**: Direct High-Precision UV Flatbed Printing.
  - Reverse-printed on the backside of clear acrylic with a white opaque backing coat, OR direct-printed on a white acrylic core with a scratch-resistant matte varnish layer.
- **Color Palette**:
  - **Background**: Crisp Studio White (`#FFFFFF`) or Deep Obsidian Black (`#111827`).
  - **Accent Colors**: Official Google Review Gold (`#FBBC05`), Google Blue (`#4285F4`), Slate Text (`#1E293B`).

## 3. NFC Chip & Shielding Specifications
- **Chip Type**: NTAG213 (144 bytes memory) or NTAG215 (504 bytes).
- **Placement**: Affixed to the backside of the acrylic sheet directly behind the `(( NFC ))` icon target using a 25mm round self-adhesive tag.
- **Anti-Metal Ferrite Shielding**:
  - If the standee is placed on metal cash registers, stainless steel counters, or POS terminals, specify **"On-Metal / Ferrite-Backed NFC Tags"**.
  - Standard NFC tags will fail to read on metal surfaces due to signal eddy current absorption. Ferrite shielding layers ensure 100% read rates.
